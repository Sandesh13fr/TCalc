# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate, including people who are new to programming, Git, or open source.

- Be patient, respectful, and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Ask questions freely and help others without ridicule or pressure.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, post sexualized content, or share someone else's private information.
- Respect issue assignments and first-time contributor reservations. Do not pressure maintainers to merge unreviewed work or bypass checks.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful public content, ask participants to change their behavior, or restrict participation for serious or repeated violations. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.

Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Use GitHub's **Report content** / **Report abuse** controls for harassment or other platform violations. Report security vulnerabilities through the process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not in public threads.
